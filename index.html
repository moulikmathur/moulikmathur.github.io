Moulik has spent four years in newsrooms and digital publications, filing everything from same-day reviews and breaking news to long-form interviews and SEO-driven guides. He's covered technology, gaming, entertainment and lifestyle beats for outlets including IGN India, Destructoid, How-To Geek, India Today Gaming and Newzera — writing under daily deadline pressure, editing others' copy, and adapting tone across news, features and social formats.

He holds an MA in Journalism from the University of Sheffield, a Postgraduate Diploma in English Journalism from the Indian Institute of Mass Communication, and a BA in English & Spanish from the University of Delhi. He's now based in London, looking to bring that range to a content, copywriting or editorial team.
